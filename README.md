# Retail Performance Analysis — Sales & Profit

An interactive Tableau dashboard analyzing retail sales and profit performance across product categories, sub-categories, geography, and time.  
Built to demonstrate exploratory analysis, distribution diagnostics, and performance benchmarking using business-relevant KPIs.

---

## 📊 Project Overview

This project explores **retail sales performance** with a focus on:
- Revenue concentration across categories
- Geographic variation in sales
- Temporal trends in sales and profit
- Distributional behavior of sales across sub-categories
- Performance versus a hypothetical growth target

The analysis is designed to mirror how analysts evaluate **commercial performance, volatility, and growth opportunities** in real-world business settings.

---

## 🧩 Dashboards & Key Analyses

### 1. Category Share of Total Sales
- Visualizes revenue concentration across major product categories
- Highlights dependence on high-performing segments

### 2. Sales Distribution by State
- Geographic comparison of sales performance
- Identifies regional demand patterns and concentration risk

### 3. Quarterly Sales & Profit Trends
- Time-series analysis of sales and profitability
- Captures seasonality, trend shifts, and volatility

### 4. Sales Distribution by Sub-Category (Log Scale)
- Box-and-whisker plots with logarithmic scaling
- Reveals dispersion, skewness, and outliers across sub-categories
- Log scale improves comparability across highly unequal ranges

### 5. Actual Sales vs Target (20% Growth Benchmark)
- Bullet chart comparing realized sales against a growth target
- Highlights sub-categories exceeding or underperforming expectations

### 6. Quarterly Sales Heatmap
- High-resolution view of sales intensity across time and sub-categories
- Enables rapid pattern detection and cross-period comparison
